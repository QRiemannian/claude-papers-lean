## 3 · Formal reading

- **The generators** (`g5_u2_d5_gens`): S̄ and T̄ are involutions (square 1, not 1); R̄ = T̄S̄ (the record's "R̄ = T̄S̄"); R̄ has order 5 (R̄⁵ = 1, R̄ ≠ 1; 5 is prime, so the order is exactly 5); and S̄R̄S̄ = R̄⁴ (= R̄⁻¹, the relation srs = r⁻¹ of AM §3.1's presentation, with r = R̄, s = S̄).
- **The image** (`g5_u2_d5_image`): the image of ⟨S, T⟩ under reduction mod 2 is read as the submonoid of 2 × 2 matrices over 𝔽₄ generated by S̄ and T̄, and stated equal (as a set) to `D5bar` = {R̄^j, R̄^j S̄ : j < 5}, which has exactly 10 elements. Reading it as the generated SUBMONOID is exact here: S̄⁻¹ = S̄ and T̄⁻¹ = T̄ (the first conjuncts), so the subgroup generated equals the submonoid generated, and the image of the group generated by S, T under a monoid map is generated by the images. Generators {S̄, T̄} as the brief and AM §2.2 write them; AM §2.3 computed the closure of {S̄, R̄} — the same submonoid, since R̄ = T̄S̄ and T̄ = R̄S̄.
- **"The dihedral group of order 10"**: read as the two theorems together — an element of order 5, an involution conjugating it to its inverse, and the generated set equal to {r^j, r^j s} with 10 distinct members. No isomorphism to mathlib's `DihedralGroup 5` is stated (that needs a group structure on the image; the relations above determine it).
- The record's two parentheticals S² = −I ≡ I and T² = [[1, 2φ], [0, 1]] ≡ I are the proof of the first conjuncts (U1's `g5_u1_r5` gives R⁵ = −I for R̄⁵ = 1), not separate statements.
- Every claim is about ten explicit 2 × 2 matrices over a four-element ring: closable by computation (STATEMENTS.md: "D₅ closure is `decide`-able").
