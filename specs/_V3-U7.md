## The definitions read against the record

- **`HeckeGrp`** = `Subgroup.closure {u : M2ˣ | (u : M2) = S ∨ (u : M2) = T}`. S and T have determinant 1 over the commutative ring ℤ[φ], so each is the value of exactly one unit (units are determined by their value); the generating set is the two units S, T, and the closure is the subgroup ⟨S, T⟩ of GL₂(ℤ[φ]). A subgroup closure is the set of finite products of the generators and their inverses, so it is the same set as the record's ⟨S, T⟩ ⊂ SL(2, ℝ) (the ambient group does not change the closure).
