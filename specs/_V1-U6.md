## The unit specifics (a)–(g)

- **(a) No φ-ring on the target.** Each §5 was checked. M4a and M3a use only `Matrix.map red_n`, set image and `Set.ncard`. M4b uses `red4` of a trace computed in `ZPhi`, then `∈ H4`. M4c, M4d and M3b are `Int.ModEq` on ℤ. Nothing multiplies on `ZMod n × ZMod n` or on a matrix over it, so the componentwise product can never be read as ℤ[φ]/n. The counts are faithful because ℤ[φ]/n ≅ (ℤ/n)² additively on {1, φ}.
- **(b) Word image vs group image.** The record's 320 and 120 are GROUP image orders (AM l. 95 "Image orders in SL", l. 100, l. 103; DO l. 108–109; SH-P2-1 l. 284). M4a and M3a count word reductions. The equality is sound:
  - the word reductions are closed under product;
  - a non-empty sub-semigroup of a finite group is a subgroup;
  - [1] and [2] give R̄S̄ and R̄²S̄, hence R̄ and then S̄, hence the whole image ⟨S̄, T̄⟩;
  - conversely every word lies in it.

  My two closures agree: 320 = 320 and 120 = 120. I sign the word-level form as faithful. The group-level `Nat.card (Subgroup.closure …)` form would need ℤ[φ]/n as a ring instance, and design note 4 keeps such group-level forms out of the word statements. I read the sources, not note 3: the "240" is (matrix, k mod 2) states at level 3, consistent with the erratum dated 10:18:27Z.
