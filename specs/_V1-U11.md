## The witness-clause decision — KEEP

`g5_u11_census_b_wit : ntr [1, 4] < 3 * phi` stays. "Fails at exactly one value" is two claims: the law fails (existence) and only at one value (the main clause). Struck, the unit would state only "at most one value" — weaker than the row. The record carries the existence at full strength: the row's Lean notes ("(b) as the remark naming its witness … 'exactly one' is printed because the record carries it at full strength"), §4 l. 51 ("the word (1, 4) has length 2 and normalized trace 3 + φ < 3φ"), SH-P2-1 l. 353 ("It fails only on (1, 4)" — "fails" existential, "only" the main clause). The facts that [1, 4] is a word, non-constant and of length 2 > 1 are closed and decidable on the literal and named in the docstring; they are not stated, and that is not a drift. (A literal existential `∃ w, IsWord w ∧ hc ∧ ntr w < 3 * phi ∧ 1 < w.length` would read "fails" word for word; it adds nothing the literal does not show, so I do not ask M for it.) U11's import list is unchanged either way.
