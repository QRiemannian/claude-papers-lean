## 3 · Formal reading

- **Main** (`g5_u2_l2`): for every word w (`IsWord w`, any length k ≥ 1), the trace x + yφ of the literal word W(w) does not have x odd and y even. Sign-symmetric (gap 2: "x odd ∧ y even" is invariant under t ↦ −t), so the literal lift loses nothing.
- **Corollary** (`g5_u2_l2_int`), the word-level form of "Θ(G(5)) ∩ ℤ ⊆ 2ℤ" / "no odd integer is a trace": if the trace of W(w) is an integer (y = 0), that integer is even. Integer traces are read as y = 0 in `ZPhi` (the pair (x, 0) is the integer x).
- **Words, not elements** (STATEMENTS.md §2 note 4; the brief: "the group-level form is U7's"): the record says "no element of G(5)"; the row covers the syllable words only. The remaining elements (±I, ±S, ±R^a, and conjugates of ± a syllable word, U7's normal form) are U7's leg.
