  - STATEMENTS asks for the exact ring form 11·|t^σ| ≤ (17 − 7φ)·t under the real order, decided by the sign of a + b√5.

  - |y| ≥ k − 1, with equality iff the word is a rotation of (1, 4^{k−1}) or (4, 1^{k−1}).

## The nine theorems, each against the record

- **V3-CLEAN `g5_u4_ba`.** Hypotheses: `IsWord w`, `∃ a ∈ w, a ≠ 1`, `∃ a ∈ w, a ≠ 4`. Conclusion: `1 ≤ y ∧ 0 ≤ x ∧ x ≤ y + 2`.
  - The hypotheses exclude exactly (1^k) and (4^k). These are the parabolic words, with t = 2.
  - On words, the complement is exactly the hyperbolic set: every other word has t ≥ 2φ > 2 by the ladder (U5), and y ≥ 1 already rules out integer traces.
  - So the domain is the record's "hyperbolic", and the conclusion is the record's inequality word for word.
- **V3-CLEAN `g5_u4_bb`.** Same hypotheses. Conclusion: `x = y + 2 ↔ ∃ a b, 1 ≤ a ∧ 1 ≤ b ∧ IsRotated w (1^a ++ 4^b)`.
  - This is AM-2′ restricted to hyperbolic words, as STATEMENTS restricts it.
  - The condition a, b ≥ 1 is forced on that domain.
  - Both directions are present, as SH-P2-1 §1 checks both.
- **V3-CLEAN `g5_u4_bb_tr`.** For a, b ≥ 1 and any rotation w of 1^a 4^b, `ntr w = ⟨2 + ab, ab⟩`. This is the family's trace (2 + ab) + abφ. No `IsWord` hypothesis is needed, since every such w is a word.
- **V3-CLEAN `g5_u4_bc`.** Same hypotheses as `g5_u4_ba`. Conclusion: `11 * rabs (conj t) ≤ (17 − 7φ) * t ∧ (11 * rabs (conj t) = (17 − 7φ) * t ↔ t = 3 + φ)`.
  - `rabs` is the real absolute value in U1's real order.
  - The inequality is STATEMENTS' exact ring form of |t^σ| ≤ ((17 − 7φ)/11)·t.
  - The iff is "the maximum attained only at t = 3 + φ", read as equality in the ring form.
  - My own reduction: (17 − 7φ)t − 11t^σ = (6x − 18y) + (21y − 7x)φ, whose real value is (3y − x)(7√5 − 5)/2. So the positive side holds iff x ≤ 3y, which follows from x ≤ y + 2 and y ≥ 1, with equality iff (x, y) = (3, 1). The negative side is strict. This agrees with SH-P2-1's monotone-θ argument.
- **V3-CLEAN `g5_u4_bc_id`.** `(4 − φ)·11 = (17 − 7φ)(3 + φ)`, the cross-multiplied form of (4 − φ)/(3 + φ) = (17 − 7φ)/11. By hand: (17 − 7φ)(3 + φ) = 51 − 4φ − 7φ² = 44 − 11φ.

- **V3-CLEAN `g5_u4_fa`.** Hypotheses: `IsWord w`, `2 ≤ length`, `∃ a ∈ w, ∃ b ∈ w, a ≠ b`. Conclusion: `(k : ℤ) − 1 ≤ |y|`. This is AM-3's floor. See note (i) on "non-constant".
